# Introduction
Welcome to the Cybersecurity Guide
As you've probably noticed, I have strong opinions that are loosely held, specifically regarding education content and cybersecurity. I am often wrong, but never in doubt. But one thing will hold true I love memes, especially the midwit meme. Whether you're a beginner or a seasoned professional, you will get something out of this roadmap. 

There are 3 core requirements to learn anything:
1. Consistency
2. Volume
3. Feedback

Back in 2017, when I broke into cybersecurity... I was studying for the CompTIA Security+ almost everyday (consistency) for about 2-3 hours (ngl, lol, I skipped every weekend), reading taking practice questions and building systems. Over the course of that 6 weeks I had studied roughly 1200-1500 practice questions, taken 4 full length practice exams, and made about 100 physical flashcards (volume). Every few weeks my "seasoned" coworkers and I would do a "whiteboard" session where for an hour I could do Q&A with them (feedback). Most of the time I would be asking about specific questions, or exact test content, but it was a great opportunity to hear from a professional WHY I got something wrong, and explain how I could reframe my thinking to get it right moving forward.

Obviously you can do 1 and 2 by yourself, but to get the full effect and add in feedback, you can join the accompying [Skool community](https://www.skool.com/cybersecurity/about), but no hard sell.
